The guided review

Review the steps

Describe one proposal, complete six tests, and use the final synthesis to see how the evidence connects.

  1. 1 Red line testSet the privacy, consent, authority, equity, and ownership conditions the proposal must meet.
  2. 2 Stress testExamine failure, unsupported output, security, reliability, accessibility, correction, and recourse.
  3. 3 Costs and benefitsCompare labor, risk, resources, maintenance, likely benefits, and a non-AI option.
  4. 4 Hidden curriculumReview changes to values, behavior, authority, knowledge, invisible work, and dependence.
  5. 5 AccountabilityIdentify who explains, audits, corrects, suspends, reviews, and eventually retires the system.
  6. 6 Internal and external reviewInvolve affected staff, participants, existing advisors, governance groups, and approvers.
  7. 7 SynthesisReview every response and map current conditions, decisions, pathways, and possible next steps.
