Nonprofit AI toolkit
A decision guide for nonprofit staff

Decide whether an AI proposal fits your organization.

Nonprofit staff review one AI proposal at a time. The guide explains each decision and keeps the reasoning in one record.

Each test records one route: proceed, negotiate and return, or walk away. Your organization makes the decision.

The review

Start with one proposal. Your answers stay in one adoption record across six tests.

2Stress testCheck failure, security, correction, and recourse.
3Costs and benefitsCompare labor, risk, maintenance, and a non-AI option.
4Hidden curriculumCheck what the tool changes about authority and work.
5AccountabilityName who reviews, explains, corrects, or stops it.
6Internal and external reviewInvolve staff and the people affected.
